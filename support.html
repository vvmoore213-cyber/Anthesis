Playing

Nothing blooms when I tap.

One ripple only makes a bud stir. It needs two rings arriving at nearly the same moment from different sides, so tap with both thumbs at once, one either side of the bud. The first pond shows two pulsing marks where to tap.

How do chains work?

A bursting bloom sends out a bright ring. Any bud close enough to that ring opens from it alone, and sends out its own. Buds placed in clusters fall together, and the chain count rises with each one.

Some flowers need more.

Water lilies need three rings instead of two. Dandelions scatter into a wide burst that reaches further than any other flower, so save them for a crowded pond. A prism bud, shimmering through every colour, opens the whole pond when it goes.

The boss flower

Once a session, from about five minutes in, the pond clears and one giant flower takes it. A ring closes in with the music and a count of 3, 2, 1 shows inside the flower. Tap the flower on NOW, as the ring meets its edge, and a layer tears away. Too early or too late and it says so, knits back, and the ring comes round again; nothing is lost. Four layers open it. There are five bosses, one each day.

Anxious mode

Choose ANXIOUS instead of URGE on the start screen. Hold a flower as you breathe in and let go as you breathe out. There are no chains, no count and no boss.

A flower is somewhere I cannot reach.

Leave it for a few seconds. When only a few flowers are left, they drift in towards open water on their own.

The clean-day count and the tank

Setting the start date

Tap the day counter on the start screen, then Your streak. You can set a start date in the past if you have already been counting.

Logging a slip

On the same screen, tap Log a slip and confirm. The count starts again from today. Your total of clean days and your longest run are both kept, and the next new mantis is counted from the total, so it keeps coming. The mantises already in your tank step out for a while and come back as the new count grows.

When do new mantises arrive?

The European mantis on day one, then the Chinese, grass, boxer, twig, budwing, dead leaf and bark mantises in the first three weeks; the giant Asian at a month; then one every ten to thirty days through the year: ant, jewelled flower, shield, unicorn, moss, conehead, lichen, Brunner's stick, spiny flower, Indian flower, thistle, ghost, wandering violin and orchid. The devil's flower mantis arrives at a year.

Each tank looks different.

Every mantis has its own vivarium, planted for where it lives. Swipe or use NEXT and PREV to move between them. Tap a mantis to see its true colour and a fact about it. SHARE SPECIMEN makes a card for that one; SHARE TANK makes one for the whole collection.

A note before my usual urge window

After a few sessions the app can see the hours your urges tend to come. In Your streak there is an option to send one quiet line fifteen minutes before. It is off unless you turn it on.

Puzzle ponds

What is par?

The fewest ripples the pond can be cleared in. Each tap is one ripple. Clearing a pond at par earns three blossoms, two if you use one extra pair of taps, and one for any clear. Tapping a flower directly makes it hum and costs nothing.

The water stilled before I finished.

All your ripples were spent with flowers still on the water. Tap Try again. Look for the bud whose bloom would reach the most neighbours, then find two tap points on opposite sides of it.

How do I unlock the next pond?

Clear the one before it with any number of blossoms.

Full bloom

What is free and what is paid?

Playing the pond, rating the urge, seeing your history and counting your clean days are free. Full bloom is one purchase, made once and kept, that opens the tank and all forty puzzle ponds. The first six ponds are free to everyone.

I bought it but the tank is locked.

Open the tank or a locked pond and tap Restore purchase. You need to be signed in to the same Apple ID you bought it with. A new phone picks it up the same way.

Refunds

Apple handles all App Store refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Sound and music

Both can be switched off from the start screen. Music keeps playing if you were already listening to something else, mixed underneath.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on your phone. Deleting the app removes all of it. See the privacy policy.
